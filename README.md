# Sales_Performance_Dashboard
Interactive Sales Dashboard built with Power BI, delivering actionable insights on revenue, profit, customer segments, and product performance to support data-driven decision-making.


## 1-Key Features

1. Sales Overview:
   - Total Sales: Displays overall revenue generated.
   - COGS (Cost of Goods Sold): Tracks total cost associated with sales.
   - Profit: Highlights total net profit.
   - Total Units Sold: Shows the volume of products sold.

2. Sales Analysis by Country:
   - Comparison of total sales across multiple countries.
   - Identifies top-performing and low-performing regions.

3. Profit by Segment:
   - Profit distribution across different customer segments:
     - Government
     - Small Business
     - Enterprise
     - Midmarket
     - Channel Partners
   - Helps in identifying the most profitable segment.

4. Time-Based Analysis:
   - Profit trends over quarters (Q1–Q4).
   - Detects seasonal patterns and growth trends.

5. Product Performance:
   - Units sold per product.
   - Total profit generated by each product.
   - Enables product-level performance evaluation.

## 2-Tools & Technologies

- SQL for data cleaning and transformation  
- Power BI for data modeling and dashboard creation  
- DAX for KPIs and calculated measures  
- Data Visualization techniques for better insights  

## 3-Questions (KPI'S)

- What is the total sales, profit, and COGS?
- Which country generates the highest sales?
- Which segment is the most profitable?
- How does profit change over time (quarterly)?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- What is the total number of units sold?
- Which segment is underperforming?
- Are there any seasonal trends in profit?
- Which country has the lowest sales performance?

## 4-Purpose

The dashboard aims to help business stakeholders understand sales performance, identify profitable segments and products, and make data-driven decisions to improve overall business efficiency and profitability.
